spine opens west into a collapsed archive and east into a more intact cistern.

chamber. Only the original pale entrance threshold banks plates/exits.

creatures from several directions. Ambient was reduced from 0.12 to 0.075;
branches retain small local sconces and the forehead gem's 4.5 m reading light.

archive has Cupcap, mite and Glarebell; cistern has a mite; the northern chamber
holds the unknown, another mite and Glarebell. Lake/vantage ecology remains.

and expanded smoke all passed. Geometry fixture checks shared hull supporting
planes, cistern access/steps, and northern gallery. Smoke visits the dark

3 hearts in the pre-art run. Art and sound follow in their own commit.

rock strata, worn pale paving, dark ceilings and blue-green water. Course UVs
map to individual blocks. Two additive tapered shafts visibly connect the
open roof to the lake; nearby Glarebells provide small local emission and the
Slatejaw's jaw warning receives a small warm local light. Ambient stays 0.075.

three painted facial expressions per species, and world-planted foot contacts.
