# Geometry rework

The sanctuary is now about 48 x 60 metres at its outer extents. The main entry
spine opens west into a collapsed archive and east into a more intact cistern.
Beyond the lake, a lower chamber leads through a dark gallery to the northern
chamber. Only the original pale entrance threshold banks plates/exits.

Masonry has dressed courses, lintels, capitals, a shore colonnade and small
traversable steps. Rock intrusions, rubble, ceiling roots, stalactites and the
irregular west ascent use the exact same point sets for convex render faces
and Box3D hulls. Masonry's inset mortar joints differ from the bounding wall
by at most 5 cm; openings, steps and structural silhouettes match collision.

The lake roof has open voids, with a surviving transverse beam and broken rock
around the edges. Local sunlight and bounce points illuminate the water and
creatures from several directions. Ambient was reduced from 0.12 to 0.075;
branches retain small local sconces and the forehead gem's 4.5 m reading light.
Exposure grading samples the same light list. Creature population stays 13:
archive has Cupcap, mite and Glarebell; cistern has a mite; the northern chamber
holds the unknown, another mite and Glarebell. Lake/vantage ecology remains.

Tests: static check 0 errors/warnings; geometry, M1, M2, hazards, exploration,
and expanded smoke all passed. Geometry fixture checks shared hull supporting
planes, cistern access/steps, and northern gallery. Smoke visits the dark
archive, lake, vantage and same entrance, ending with 3 plates / 878 crowns /
3 hearts in the pre-art run. Art and sound follow in their own commit.
